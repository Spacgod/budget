Privacy & Terms

Short version: the app keeps your business information on your computer, and we never see it. Here are the details in plain English.

The app

  • Your data stays on your computer. Clients, invoices, schedules, staff lists and everything else you enter are saved in a file in your Documents folder. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No account, no tracking. The app has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.
  • License keys are checked offline. The app checks your key on your computer, without the internet.
  • You’re in charge of your records. Because everything stays with you, keeping your computer secure and keeping backups is up to you. The app makes a backup every day, and you can export one any time.

This website

  • Forms. When you use the contact form or the “forgot to add your email” form, the details you type (such as your name, email and message) are sent to us through Formspree. We use them only to reply to you and send your license key, and we don’t sell or share them.
  • Payments. Payments are made through Cash App or Venmo, under their own terms and privacy policies. We see the payment details those apps show us (such as your name and the note you add), and we use them only to match your payment and send your key.
  • Fonts. This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download them.
  • No cookies from us. This site doesn’t set tracking or advertising cookies.

Your license

  • What you get. A one-time payment of $20 gets you a license key for your business, with every feature and one year of free updates. The version you have keeps working after that; you don’t need to pay again to keep using it.
  • Where you can use it. One license covers one business, on the computers that business uses. Please don’t share your key outside your business.
  • Free trial. You can try every feature free for 30 days before buying. After the trial, the app becomes read-only until you enter a key; nothing you’ve entered is deleted.
  • Refunds. If it isn’t right for you, contact us within 14 days of paying for a refund.
  • As-is. We work hard to make the app reliable, but it’s provided as-is, without warranties. Please keep backups of important records, and check figures such as taxes and payroll with a professional.
