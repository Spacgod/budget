Updates

What’s new

To update, close Southstar Business and run the newest installer from the download page. Your clients, invoices, lists, settings and license stay exactly where they are.

Version 0.2.0

October 4, 2026

New

  • Money in & out reports: money in vs money out by month, profit or loss each month, running totals, and where your money comes from and goes.
  • Money chart on Home for the last six months.
  • Make it yours: three layouts (sidebar, icon rail, top bar), text size, square or rounded corners, and classic or modern headings.
  • Choose your Home page: show, hide and reorder its sections.
  • Table, Cards and Board views for every custom list. Drag cards between columns on the board.
  • Savings goals: set a target on any budget category, see your progress, and get a “save this much a month” plan with a one-click Budget it button.
  • Help & how-to: step-by-step guides inside the app with a “Take me there” button. Press F1 or ? anytime.
  • Easier bank accounts: big Money in, Money out and Move money buttons, a card for each account with its balance, and Update a balance to match your bank app in one step.
  • One calendar for everything: tasks, invoices, bills and shifts now show on the dates they’re due. Today is highlighted, and you can click any day to add something.
  • Easier invoices and clients: a Mark paid button on every unpaid invoice, “5 days late” and “In 3 days” hints, short dates, no more sideways scrolling, and a + Invoice button on each client.
  • Easier to find your way: Settings has jump links to each section, every page opens at the top, Escape closes forms, and the sidebar fits more on smaller screens.
  • Friendlier budget: a plain-English “How the budget works” guide, and buttons that explain themselves.
  • Easier license keys: a “Paste key” button, and keys are found even inside a pasted email.

Fixed

  • Money in & out no longer counts old paid invoices from before you added your bank account as new money. That money is already part of your starting balance.
  • Home no longer shows alerts for tools you’ve switched off.
  • Top services group lines like “RN shifts, week of Sep 10” together instead of listing each date separately.
