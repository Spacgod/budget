Questions

Good to know

Where is my information stored?

In a file called workspace.json in Documents\Southstar Business, with a daily backup in the backups folder next to it. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Do I need the internet?

No. The app runs entirely on your computer. You only need a connection to email an invoice or open a payment link.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes. Add as many businesses as you like and switch between them from the top of the sidebar. Each keeps its own clients, invoices, lists and settings.

Can I move my spreadsheet into it?

Yes. Save your sheet as CSV and import it into any custom list; columns are matched to fields by name. Bank statements import as CSV, OFX or QIF.

Is it safe for staff and client records?

Your records stay on your own computer and are never uploaded. Keep your computer password-protected, and leave detailed health information out of notes.

How do I get a license key?

Pay $20 with Cash App or Venmo (see Pricing) and put your email address in the payment note. Your key arrives by email. Paste it into the app by clicking the license badge in the sidebar.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. The app becomes read-only: you can still open, search, print and export everything. Enter a license key to keep adding and changing things.

Does it work on a Mac?

Southstar Business is available for Windows 10 and 11 today.
